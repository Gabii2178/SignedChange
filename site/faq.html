FAQ

Do my clients need to install anything or make an account?

No install or account is needed for the other party. Write the change, then send a private one-time signing link or hand them the device. Signing links expire after 14 days.

Where is my data stored?

Documents, signatures, and photos you attach are stored so both people can download the same PDF, for up to two years. Your account is a private key in the browser. The public verify page shows status, names, dates, and fingerprints, not signatures, amounts, or contact details. You can delete a document you own.

Is a SignedChange PDF legally binding?

It is a written, signed, and dated record. Electronic signatures are recognised in many places, and rules vary. SignedChange does not verify identity and is not legal advice. It does not issue a qualified electronic signature.

How much does it cost?

The first 3 documents on an account are free. Pro Monthly is $9 USD per month. Pro Lifetime is a one-time payment of $49 USD. Both are described with a 7-day money-back note in the terms.

How can I pay?

Through PayPal, including card checkout on PayPal’s page where guest checkout is available. That is the payment method in the app.

What if verification cannot confirm a document?

The page says it cannot confirm the document. It does not show an older status as if it were current. A record that does not match its fingerprint is rejected.

Who is SignedChange for?

Contractors, freelancers, employers, agencies, and other service businesses whose agreements change after the original deal.

How do I contact you?

Email termschange@gmail.com.
