The service

SignedChange is a tool for creating signed records of agreed changes. You are responsible for the content of your documents and for checking that electronic signatures meet the requirements of your country and situation. SignedChange is not legal advice.

Free and Pro

The free plan includes 3 documents per account (and a limited number per network each month). Pro Monthly is $9 USD per month, billed by PayPal until you cancel; after you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the month you paid for. Pro Lifetime is a one-time payment of $49 USD for continued use of the Pro features while the service operates.

Payments and refunds

Payments are processed only by PayPal (card or PayPal balance on PayPal's secure page). 7-day money-back guarantee: email termschange@gmail.com within 7 days of a payment for a full refund of that payment; Pro access from a refunded payment ends automatically. Cancel a monthly subscription any time in your PayPal account under Payments → Automatic payments.

Acceptable use

Do not use SignedChange for fraud, forgery or to create documents someone did not actually agree to.

Liability

The service is provided "as is". To the extent allowed by law, we are not liable for indirect losses or disputes between you and the other party to your agreement.
