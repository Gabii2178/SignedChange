The problem

Extra work gets agreed on site and never written down. At payment time, the other person does not remember the same conversation.

A price changes in a message thread. Later, neither side can point to the number they both accepted.

A date moves. The job still has the old deadline on the original paper, and the argument starts there.

How it works

1. Write

Describe the change, the people, and any money or date that moved.

2. Review

Read the whole change before a signature. The other person does not need an account.

3. Sign

Sign on this device, or send a one-time link. They can also decline and send a reason.

4. Download

Both sides can download the same PDF. A public page shows status and fingerprints, not the private text of the deal.

What you keep

Names
Both parties, with optional company and role.
The change
The description you wrote, stored with the document.
Amount
Direction, tax, and an optional new total. The server recomputes the figures.
Signatures
A drawn or typed signature image from each person who signs.
A verify page
Status, names, dates, and fingerprints. No email, amount, or signature image.

This is a signed record. It is not a qualified electronic signature and it is not legal advice.

Trust

  • The signing link is the other person’s access. It expires, and the owner can cancel it.
  • The owner can delete a document. A deleted document is not shown as valid.
  • If the service cannot confirm the record, verification says it is unavailable instead of showing an old status.
  • If the copy does not match the stored record, verification rejects it.

FAQ

Do my clients need an account?

No. They open the signing link, review the change, and sign or decline. They do not see a price table before the agreement.

How much does it cost?

The first 3 documents on an account are free. Pro Monthly is $9 USD per month. Pro Lifetime is $49 USD once. Payments go through PayPal.

Is the PDF a certified signature?

No. It is a written, dated record with the signatures you captured. Rules for electronic signatures differ by country. SignedChange does not verify identity and is not legal advice.
