The problem

"Can you also do this while you're here?" You agree, do the work, and at payment time they "don't remember agreeing to that." Verbal changes are where money and trust get lost — on job sites, in offices and in client projects.

How it works

1. Write the change

Describe the extra work, new scope, schedule or pay change, and the agreed price.

2. Get it signed

Send a private link by WhatsApp or email so they sign on their own phone, or hand over your phone to sign on the spot. No app or login for them.

3. Both get the PDF

A dated PDF with both signatures and an audit trail. Each side downloads the same copy.

Frequently asked questions

Do my clients need to install anything or make an account?

No. You open the app in any phone or desktop browser, type the change, then either send your client a private signing link (WhatsApp or email) so they sign on their own phone, or hand them your device to sign with a finger.

Where is my data stored?

On SignedChange's secure server (Cloudflare), so both people can download the same signed PDF. Only you (with your private account key on your device) and the person holding the one-time signing link can open a document. We never sell or share your documents, and you can delete any document from My documents.

Is a SignedChange PDF legally binding?

It is a written, signed and dated record of what both sides agreed, which is far stronger evidence than a verbal promise. Electronic signatures are recognised in many countries (for example under the US ESIGN Act and UK law), but rules vary, so check with a local professional for high-value or regulated work.
