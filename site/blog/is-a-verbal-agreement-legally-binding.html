Is a verbal agreement legally binding?

Published 2026-11-02 · SignedChange Guides

Short answer: in many countries, many verbal agreements can be legally binding. The bigger problem is usually not whether the agreement exists, but proving what was agreed. This plain-English guide explains the general principles, the common exceptions, and the practical steps that protect you.

Important: laws vary by country and situation. This is general information, not legal advice.

What generally makes an agreement a contract

In many legal systems, a contract generally needs a few things: an offer, acceptance of that offer, something of value exchanged by both sides (for example work for money), an intention to be bound, and parties who are able to agree. Notice that "written on paper" is often not on that list. That's why a phone call or handshake can create a real agreement.

Common exceptions that usually need writing

Many countries require certain agreements to be in writing, and sometimes signed or witnessed. Common examples include sales of land or property, some leases, guarantees for someone else's debt, and agreements that can't be completed within a certain time. Employment, consumer and tenancy rules may also require written terms. Check your local rules for anything significant.

The real problem: proof

Even when a verbal agreement is valid, a dispute becomes one person's word against another's. Who said what price? Was the extra work included? What was the deadline? Without writing, a court or mediator has to decide whose memory is more believable.

Evidence that can help includes messages, emails, invoices, payments already made, witnesses and the way both sides behaved. But a signed written record is the clearest proof. Learn how to put a verbal agreement in writing.

Verbal changes to a written contract

A very common situation: there's a written contract, then the work changes verbally. Some contracts say changes must be in writing. Even when they don't, a verbal change is hard to prove. The safest habit is to record every change in a short signed note, such as a change order or contract amendment.

Are electronic signatures valid?

Many countries recognise electronic signatures for most everyday agreements; for example, the US ESIGN Act and UK law accept them in many cases. There are exceptions (such as some property and family matters), so check local rules for important deals. For everyday work changes, an electronic signature on a dated record is usually far better than nothing.

How to protect yourself in practice

  • Write down every agreement and change on the same day.
  • Include who, what, price, timing and what's excluded.
  • Get both signatures, even electronically.
  • Keep copies both sides can access.
  • For large or risky deals, use a professionally drafted contract.

Why people still rely on verbal agreements

If written agreements are so much safer, why do people keep agreeing things verbally? Mostly speed and comfort. Writing feels formal, slow, or like a sign of distrust, especially with friends, family, long-term clients or small amounts. On a job site or during a quick call, nobody wants to stop and draft a document.

The answer isn't to make everything a long contract. It's to make writing things down as fast as saying them. A short record with names, the change, the price and two signatures captures nearly everything that matters. When it takes a minute and can be signed on a phone, the excuse disappears, and the conversation shifts from "do you trust me?" to "let's both keep a copy".

That's exactly the habit that turns a verbal promise into evidence, without changing how friendly the relationship feels.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sue over a verbal agreement?

In many places you can bring a claim based on a verbal agreement, but you'll need to prove its terms. A local professional can tell you whether your situation qualifies.

Is a text or WhatsApp message a written agreement?

Messages can be good evidence that an agreement exists, and in some places can count as writing. But they're often incomplete about price and scope.

How long is a verbal agreement valid?

It depends on what was agreed and local law, including time limits for bringing claims. Ask a local professional for specific cases.

Does a witness make a verbal agreement binding?

A witness can help prove what was said, but doesn't usually change whether the agreement is binding.
