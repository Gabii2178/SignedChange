About SignedChange

Most agreements change after they are made — usually by a quick conversation. SignedChange was built so those changes get written down and signed in under a minute, by both sides, on the device already in your pocket.

Our principles

  • Simple: no accounts, no training.
  • Private: both people sign on their own phones and get the same PDF.
  • Fair: a written record protects both parties, not just one.
  • Affordable: free to start, then $9/month or $49 one-time for Pro.
