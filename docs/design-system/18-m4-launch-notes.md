# Milestone 4 launch notes (internal)

English is the only interface language in this milestone. The language menu shows English and one note that more languages may come later. It does not list unavailable languages. Stored choices other than English are reset to English. Agreement language is still the separate document field. Generated translation blocks are removed from the page. The English articles, terms, and privacy text remain.

Multilingual coverage is not complete and is not advertised.

The template library is a set of editable starting points across freelancers, software, construction and property, employment, agencies, commerce, payment arrangements in the agreement, education and events, and logistics. They are not legal advice and they do not add hidden obligations. The blank starting point leaves the change fields empty.

These notes are not published on the site and they are not a campaign. They describe the redesign that is in the working tree. Do not claim a qualified signature, a legal certification, or any price other than Free, Pro Monthly at $9, and Pro Lifetime at $49 once.

## What a visitor can do

- Read what SignedChange is: a two-party record of a change, then a signature, then a PDF.
- Open the app and create a record in five steps without a password.
- Send the existing one-time signing link. The other person reviews before any upgrade prompt.
- Check a document ID on the verify page.

## Accurate lines that can be reused later

- “When agreements change, make it official.”
- “Put the change in writing. Get both sides to sign. Keep a record you can both open.”
- “The other person does not need an account.”
- “Your first 3 documents are free. Pro Monthly is $9 a month. Pro Lifetime is $49 once.”

## Do not say

- That the record is legally binding in every country, or that it is a qualified electronic signature.
- That SignedChange stores a password or can reset a lost key.
- Any $5 monthly price, any yearly plan, or a $60 comparison. Those stay in `16-proposed-pricing.md`.
