# Brand personality

Positioning, from the strategic brief: “When agreements change, make it official.” The PDF is the output. The product is the process that gets both people to the same terms.

## Personality

| Trait | What it looks like | What it refuses |
|---|---|---|
| Dependable | Navy structure, quiet paper surfaces, one obvious next step | Alarmist red, legal-seal decoration |
| Professional | Clear labels, tabular numbers, a readable agreement | Clip-art, stock handshakes |
| Modern | Flat geometry, short steps, mobile-first spacing | Gradients, heavy drop shadows |
| Premium | Consistent type, alignment, and empty space | Gold foil, extra ornaments |
| Friendly | Plain sentences, “the other party”, not “counterparty” | Cute illustrations, jokes in errors |
| Simple | One primary action per view | A second product for employers |

Audience stays balanced: contractors, freelancers, employers, clients, and service businesses share one wizard. Do not split the interface by industry. Templates may pre-fill words. They are not separate apps.

## Voice

- Say what happened: “Waiting for signature”, “Declined”, “We can’t confirm this document right now.”
- Do not say the signature is a qualified electronic signature or a legal certification.
- Do not say a link was “sent” by SignedChange. Sharing is a local WhatsApp, mail, or copy action. The server does not record an invitation event.
- A draft saved in the browser is not a document status. Call it “Draft on this device.”

## What already works and should survive

`site/styles.css` already uses a blue bar (`#0f4c81`), an amber button (`#f6a609`), and a paper card (`#f4f7fb`). The live PNG mark is a navy S, an amber C, and a check. Keep that recognition. The long single-page form in `site/app.html` is the thing the five-step flow replaces in Milestone 4, not in this milestone.
