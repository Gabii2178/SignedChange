# Pricing supersession

This note supersedes the **proposed presentation** of prices in section 6 of the root file `SignedChange 2.0` and in the earlier Milestone 3 pages that repeated those prices.

The root brief is left unchanged as the historical owner text. Where it says Pro Monthly is $9 per month and Pro Lifetime is $49 once, that describes the **source product as it existed when the brief was written**. It is not the approved proposed display.

The approved proposed display is in [16-proposed-pricing.md](16-proposed-pricing.md). It is not for sale. Wiring it to PayPal is a separate migration, listed in [17-billing-dependencies.md](17-billing-dependencies.md).

Do not read a later `$49` in the proposed yearly card as the old Lifetime price. They are different offers that happen to use the same number.
